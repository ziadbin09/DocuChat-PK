# Claims and Refunds Policy

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when it has had no tracking scan for 10 days. Merchants must file a lost parcel claim within 21 days of the booking date. Claims filed after that window are rejected.

## Compensation

Compensation for a lost or damaged parcel is the declared value or 15,000 PKR, whichever is lower. Parcels insured through Kohsar Shield are covered up to 500,000 PKR. Shipping fees are refunded in full for lost parcels.

## Damaged parcels

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the parcel and the packaging. Claims are refused when the item was not packed according to the packaging guide, for example glass shipped without bubble wrap.

## Late deliveries

When a Zone A or Zone B parcel is delivered more than 2 working days after the promised date, the merchant receives a 50 percent refund of the shipping fee. Delays caused by weather, strikes or road closures are excluded.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are credited to the merchant wallet within 7 working days. Merchants can withdraw the wallet balance to their bank account once it reaches 1,000 PKR.

## Disputes

A merchant who disagrees with a claim decision can open a dispute within 14 days. Disputes are reviewed by a claims supervisor who was not involved in the first decision, and the outcome is final.
