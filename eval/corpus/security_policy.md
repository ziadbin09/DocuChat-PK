# IT Security Policy

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 180 days. Passwords must never be shared, written down or reused across systems. The company password manager is the only approved place to store credentials.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and the merchant dashboard admin console. Authenticator apps are required; SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal phones may access company email only after enrolling in mobile device management. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour.

## Remote access

Internal systems are reachable from outside the office only through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN switched on.

## Incident reporting

Suspected phishing emails are forwarded to security@kohsar.example. A confirmed security incident must be reported to the Security Lead within 4 hours. Customer data breaches are escalated to the CEO on the same day.

## Data handling

Customer addresses and phone numbers are classified as confidential. Confidential data must not be copied to USB drives or personal cloud storage. Records of delivered parcels are kept for 5 years and then deleted.

## Software

Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Security updates are applied within 14 days of release.
