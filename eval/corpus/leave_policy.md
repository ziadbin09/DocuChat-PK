# Leave Policy

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Annual leave

Confirmed employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted on form HR-12 at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Up to 7 unused days carry over to the next year; any remaining balance lapses on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over and cannot be encashed.

## Casual leave

Casual leave is 8 days per year and is meant for urgent personal matters. No more than 2 casual days may be taken in a row, and casual leave cannot be joined with annual leave.

## Maternity and paternity leave

Maternity leave is 16 weeks at full pay. Paternity leave is 10 working days at full pay and must be taken within 8 weeks of the birth. Both require form HR-15 with supporting documents.

## Hajj leave

Employees with at least 3 years of service may take a one-time Hajj leave of 30 days at full pay. This leave is separate from the annual leave balance.

## Leave encashment

Unused annual leave is encashed only when an employee leaves the company, at the rate of basic salary divided by 26 for each day.
