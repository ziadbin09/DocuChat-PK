# Travel and Expense Policy

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Submitting claims

Expense claims are submitted on form FIN-27 with original receipts within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted late are paid only with written approval from the Finance Director. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## Approval limits

A line manager can approve claims up to 25,000 PKR. Claims between 25,000 and 100,000 PKR need the department head. Anything above 100,000 PKR must be approved by the Finance Director before the money is spent.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance for domestic travel is 3,500 PKR per day. Hotel stays are capped at 15,000 PKR per night in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad and 9,000 PKR per night in other cities. International travel allowances are set case by case by Finance.

## Fuel and mileage

Employees using a personal car for company work are paid 38 PKR per kilometre. Motorcycle mileage is paid at 14 PKR per kilometre. Trips must be recorded in the mileage log with start and end odometer readings.

## Air travel

All flights are booked through the travel desk in economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs the CEO's approval.

## Non-reimbursable items

Traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges and lost personal items are never reimbursed.
