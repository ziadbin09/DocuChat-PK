# Kohsar Couriers Employee Handbook

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Working hours

The standard working week is 45 hours, Monday to Friday, from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm with a one-hour lunch break. Warehouse staff work in rotating shifts published every Thursday for the following week. Employees who arrive more than 15 minutes late three times in a month receive a written reminder from their line manager.

## Probation

Every new employee serves a probation period of 90 days. During probation either side may end the contract with 7 days of notice. At the end of probation the line manager completes a confirmation review, and confirmed employees move to a 30-day notice period.

## Remote work

Office-based staff may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. Remote days must be logged in the PeopleDesk portal before 10:00 am on the day. Customer support agents and warehouse staff are not eligible for remote work because their roles need on-site equipment.

## Dress code

Riders and warehouse staff must wear the company-issued green uniform and a helmet or safety shoes as their role requires. Office staff follow a smart casual dress code. Uniforms are replaced free of charge once every 12 months.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in January and July. Salary increments are decided after the July review and take effect from 1 September. An employee rated "needs improvement" in two consecutive reviews is placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by a department head. Approved overtime is paid at 2 times the hourly rate on working days and 3 times the hourly rate on public holidays.
