# Shipping Rates and Delivery FAQ

*Kohsar Couriers is a fictional company. This document is sample data for DocuChat.*

## Delivery zones and times

Zone A covers deliveries within the same city and takes 1 working day. Zone B covers deliveries between major cities and takes 2 to 3 working days. Zone C covers remote and northern areas and takes 5 to 7 working days. Deliveries are not made on Sundays or public holidays.

## Rates

A Zone A parcel costs 250 PKR for the first kilogram. Zone B costs 400 PKR and Zone C costs 650 PKR for the first kilogram. Each additional kilogram costs 90 PKR in every zone.

## Size and weight limits

The maximum weight for a single parcel is 30 kg. No side of the parcel may be longer than 120 cm. Heavier or larger shipments go through Kohsar Freight, which quotes separately.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery (COD) is available in Zones A and B. The COD fee is 2 percent of the collected amount with a minimum of 100 PKR. Collected cash is paid out to merchants every Tuesday and Friday.

## Failed deliveries

A rider makes 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel is held at the nearest hub for 5 days and then returned to the sender. The return is charged at 50 percent of the original shipping fee.

## Prohibited items

Kohsar Couriers does not carry cash, jewellery, firearms, liquids over 1 litre, or perishable food.

## Tracking

Every parcel has a 12-digit tracking number that starts with KC. Tracking updates appear within 30 minutes of each scan.
